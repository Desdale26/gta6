# Chasing the Firecrest: beat sheets, Act 4 "The Last Lap" (d24 to d30 and the three endings)

Beat sheets for the Act 4 writers (4a: d24 to d27, 4b: d28 to d30 and the endings). The plot, places, mechanics and flags come from `docs/DLC_BIBLE.md` sections 7, 8 and 9 and are not changed here; this file turns each outline into ordered steps with the engine step type, the places, who is present, what the player does and the lines that must land. Lines marked **(keep)** are fixed; everything else is a guide the scripter writes in full. The one riddle in this act (Ending C's new trail) is given in full and must not be edited. Same conventions as `beats-act1.md`, `beats-act2.md` and `beats-act3.md`.

Conventions used below:
- Step types are the engine's (`scene`, `blackout`, `phone`, `text`, `goto`, `spawnCar`, `getIn`, `spawn`, `join`, `leave`, `kill`, `survive`, `protect`, `chase`, `follow`, `race`, `deliver`, `collect`, `destroy`, `heat`, `loseHeat`, `choice`, `if`, `setFlag`, `music`, `slowmo`, `setTime`, `fade`, `teleport`, `camera`, `timer`, `wait`, `reward`, and the DLC's `fly` and `land`).
- Place ids are from `dlc.js` (story places) or the built-in landmarks (bible 6.1). Places in neither are marked **[NEW]** and listed in "NEW PLACES NEEDED" at the end. Act 2's proposed `speedway_grid`, `speedway_turn1`, `speedway_back_straight`, `speedway_turn3` (beats-act2 NEW PLACES) are reused here and marked **[act2]**.
- Vehicles use the bible's mission ids and types (6.3): `firecrest` flame `ironclad` 0xe0481c; `toby_pickup` green `mesa` 0x1f3a26; `learner` pale-blue `pipit` 0x78b8e8; `crash_tender` `firetruck` 0xc8202a; `gs_car1..n` teal `sovereign` 0x13b5b1; `gs_suv` teal `tern`; `gs_truck1..n` `bulwark` 0xdcdad2; `transporter` teal `hauler` 0x13b5b1; `war_tank` `bastion`; `doughnut_van` cream `porter` 0xefefea; `hob_skylark` yellow `skylark` 0xf2c500; `dash_heli` purple `sparrow` 0x3b1558; `castellane_racer` gold `zephyr` 0xd29a1c; Act 2's `film_lowloader` (`hauler`, cream 0xefe6d0, SUNREEL STUDIOS on the doors).
