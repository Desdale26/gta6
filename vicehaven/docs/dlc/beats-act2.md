# Chasing the Firecrest: beat sheets, Act 2 "Hiding in Plain Sight" (d09 to d16)

Beat sheets for the Act 2 writers (2a: d09 to d12, 2b: d13 to d16). The plot, places, mechanics and flags come from `docs/DLC_BIBLE.md` section 9 and are not changed here; this file turns each outline into ordered steps with the engine step type, the places, who is present, what the player does and the lines that must land. Lines marked **(keep)** are fixed; everything else is a guide the scripter writes in full. Riddles are quoted word for word from the bible's canonical table and must not be edited. Same conventions as `beats-act1.md`.

Conventions used below:
- Step types are the engine's (`scene`, `blackout`, `phone`, `text`, `goto`, `spawnCar`, `getIn`, `spawn`, `join`, `leave`, `kill`, `survive`, `protect`, `chase`, `follow`, `race`, `deliver`, `collect`, `destroy`, `heat`, `loseHeat`, `choice`, `if`, `setFlag`, `music`, `slowmo`, `setTime`, `fade`, `teleport`, `camera`, `timer`, `wait`, `reward`, and the DLC's `fly` and `land`).
- Place ids are from `dlc.js` (story places), the built-in landmarks (bible 6.1) or the airport's own registered places (`airport_apron`, `airport_gate`, `airport_hangars`, `airport_helipad` in `js/airfield.js`). Places in none of those are marked **[NEW]** and listed in "NEW PLACES NEEDED" at the end.
- Vehicles use the bible's mission ids and types (6.3): `pip_car` white `drifter` 0xf1f0ea; `learner` pale-blue `pipit` 0x78b8e8; `dash_van` purple `porter` 0x3b1558; `gs_car1..n` teal `sovereign` 0x13b5b1; `gs_suv` teal `tern`; `gs_truck` `bulwark` 0xdcdad2; `heritage_car` yellow `vireo` 0xf2c500; `castellane_racer` gold `zephyr` 0xd29a1c; `replica` `ironclad` 0xe0481c; `charter` `albatross` 0x1fa3a3; `toby_pickup` green `mesa` 0x1f3a26. One new id: `film_lowloader` (`hauler`, cream 0xefe6d0), the film unit's low-loader Chet drives in d14.
- Guest Services drivers are spawned as the act files do it, with a `driver()`-style helper: `{ id, char: 'gs_staff', faction: 'guest_services', at, behavior: 'drive', car, to: <target car id> }`, hostile unless noted.
- "Banter" = the `say` lines on a drive step; the topic is given, the scripter writes 6 to 12 lines.
- Running tallies through Act 2 (keep them consistent): **Pip calls Jay "Jay"** from here on (first used at the end of d08). **Milo calls him "Jay"** (since d05), with obvious pride. **Scoreboard** (parts): wheel, bird, clock (Act 1) = 3; carburettor (d09) = 4; headlamps (d11, a pair counts as one part) = 5; d13 the clock and the carburettor go "into custody" (Milo refuses to take points off); d16 they come back. Lockwood finds in Act 2: **garden gnome (d09), lifeguard's whistle (d11)**. **Subscribers:** with `dlc_blog_on`, 900 at d11 and 12,000 at d16; with `dlc_blog_off`, Milo reads from the card on his wall ("exclusive"). **Comment cards in Jay's glovebox:** 4 at the end of Act 1; 5 (d11), 6 (d13), 7 (d14). **Monty's wrong names:** d10 only in this act. **Hob's first mark:** 4 (d16). **June's "Serious fault":** d09, crossed out. **Jay says "damn"** about once a job, often to a car. **Quill is carsick** whenever he's in a car (d10 only, by report).
