# Chasing the Firecrest: beat sheets, Act 3 "Isla Paloma" (d17 to d23)

Beat sheets for the Act 3 writers (3a: d17 to d20, 3b: d21 to d23). The plot, places, mechanics and flags come from `docs/DLC_BIBLE.md` section 9 and are not changed here; this file turns each outline into ordered steps with the engine step type, the places, who is present, what the player does and the lines that must land. Lines marked **(keep)** are fixed; everything else is a guide the scripter writes in full. Riddles are quoted word for word from the bible's canonical table and must not be edited. Same conventions as `beats-act1.md` and `beats-act2.md`.

Conventions used below:
- Step types are the engine's (`scene`, `blackout`, `phone`, `text`, `goto`, `spawnCar`, `getIn`, `spawn`, `join`, `leave`, `kill`, `survive`, `protect`, `chase`, `follow`, `race`, `deliver`, `collect`, `destroy`, `heat`, `loseHeat`, `choice`, `if`, `setFlag`, `music`, `slowmo`, `setTime`, `fade`, `teleport`, `camera`, `timer`, `wait`, `reward`, and the DLC's `fly` and `land`).
- Place ids are from `dlc.js` (story places) or the built-in landmarks (bible 6.1). Places in neither are marked **[NEW]** and listed in "NEW PLACES NEEDED" at the end.
- Vehicles use the bible's mission ids and types (6.3): `doughnut_van` cream `porter` 0xefefea; `hob_skylark` yellow `skylark` 0xf2c500; `club_sparrow` white `sparrow` 0xf2f2ee; `transporter` teal `hauler` 0x13b5b1; `gs_car1..n` teal `sovereign` 0x13b5b1; `gs_suv` teal `tern` 0x13b5b1; `dash_van` purple `porter` 0x3b1558. One new id: `party_car` (`sovereign`, pearl white 0xf2f2ee, a valet ticket on the mirror), the party guest's car Jay takes in d20.
- Guest Services drivers are spawned as the act files do it, with a `driver()`-style helper: `{ id, char: 'gs_staff', faction: 'guest_services', at, behavior: 'drive', car, to: <target car id> }`, hostile unless noted.
- "Banter" = the `say` lines on a drive or flight step; the topic is given, the scripter writes 6 to 12 lines.
- **Act 3 framing (bible):** from d17 to d26 the Paloma Bridge is watched ("Welcome Checks") and the parts live in Hob's hangar (`hob_hangar`). In jobs the island is reached by air or by Hob's van; Jay may drive over the bridge in free roam. Every Act 3 job starts on the island.
- Running tallies through Act 3 (keep them consistent):
  - **Scoreboard (parts):** 5 at the end of Act 2 (wheel, bird, clock, carburettor, headlamps-as-a-pair). Gear stick (d19) = 6. Ignition coil (d21) = 7, which is all seven parts. d23: "the last part is the part that's me": Milo doesn't know how to score a driver.
  - **Lockwood finds in Act 3:** the teddy bear (d19) only. Dash is otherwise busy being handcuffed (d20).
  - **Subscribers (blog on):** 12,000 at d16; still about 12,000 in d19 ("and a bit"); **80,000** at d21 (the festival bell video). With `dlc_blog_off` Milo reads from the card on his wall instead, every time.
  - **Comment cards in Jay's glovebox:** 7 at the end of Act 2; **8** in d20 (tucked in Dash's hatband, "How would you rate your experience of being restrained today?"). In d23 (blog off) Ottoline has run out of cards: "I'll post one." That leaves d28 to make nine.
  - **Hob's marks:** 4 (d16). **7** (d18, first proper lesson). d20: "Bailing out isn't a landing. No mark." **8** (d22, night landing; the bible's line "Night landing. Eight. Don't let it go to your head.").
  - **Quill:** seasick on the ferry and carsick in every car; **fine in aircraft** (d20 Sparrow). He gets a scholarly wrong answer for R9 (d21) and R10 (d22).
  - **Dess:** speaker `odie` in d17 and d22 and in d23 up to Toby's "Hello, Dess."; speaker `dess` from her first reply on. Never `dess` before d23.
  - **"And mind the road":** Pip says it in d18 (to Jay, climbing into the Skylark), d21 (to Jay on the radio, "mind the... roofs") and d23 (from the van, leaving the fort). Dess does **not** say it in Act 3: at the end of d23 she hears Pip say it, asks "Where did you get that?", and Pip says "Off your riddles." Dess saying it to June's face is saved for d30.
  - **Three taps:** Odie taps the café counter three times in d17 (seen; nobody comments; Jay looks). Dess taps the powder-store bricks three times and, at the end of d23, the door frame. Toby explains the habit in d22 ("So it knows I'm here.").
  - **Jay says "damn"** about once a job, often to a vehicle. Milo calls him "Jay"; Pip calls him "Jay"; Toby calls him "son"; Hob calls him "son"; Odie/Dess calls him "young man" (d17) until the end of d23 ("Jay, is it?"), then "Jay".
  - **Milo is never in danger:** in Act 3 he is always on the phone or a video call from Halcyon (he is on the mainland all act).

## d17_contraband: Contraband

- **id** `d17_contraband` · **act** 3 · **title** Contraband · **giver** `hob` · **start** `hob_hangar` · **requires** `d16_four_out_of_ten` (default) · **time** 7.5 · **estMinutes** 9
- **reward** `{ money: 1200 }` (plus "a doughnut", in the pass caption only) · **failIf** `['wrecked:doughnut_van', 'dead:hob']`
- **summary** "Isla Paloma has one bakery, and Castellane owns it. Hob Finnegan has a van."
- **Core:** timed delivery. **Cast:** jay, hob, pip, gs_staff (checkpoint), ines, odie, milo (phone), prof_quill (background on Milo's call). **Reads:** `dlc_blog_on` / `dlc_blog_off` (one Milo line).

2. `camera: 'paloma_field'`, 4 s, caption "Isla Paloma. Paloma Field. Tuesday, half past seven in the morning." One runway, three hangars, a new windsock, a flag with a green bird on it.
3. `spawnCar` `doughnut_van` at `hob_hangar` (cream `porter`, PALOMA FLYING CLUB on the side). `spawn` hob and pip at `hob_hangar`, idle.
4. `scene` at `hob_hangar`, cast hob, pip, jay. The hangar mouth: Hob's Skylark, the club's white Sparrow, a kettle, forty boxes of doughnuts stacked like sandbags. Pip has come over on the first ferry with a sports bag (the wheel, the bird, the headlamps) and is laying the five parts out on a doughnut box like a crime scene. Hob explains the island while making tea: Castellane owns every bakery on Isla Paloma; by "hotel bylaw" only Castellane Croissants may be sold; the old folk of Old Paloma want a doughnut. **(keep)** Hob: "Castellane's got the croissants. I've got the doughnuts. That's the war, son. That's the whole war." The deal: the parts live in the hangar ("Nobody looks in a hangar. Too many doughnuts."), and the rent is Jay. Jay: "I don't do deliveries." Hob: "You landed an airliner on my driveway. You do deliveries now." Pip reads **R8** aloud again from the Kwik Pik envelope, word for word, for the player's benefit (still unsolved): "I'm in a box where nobody wins, / beside a wheel that turns and spins. / Many have tried with a coin and a claw; / the wheel goes round, and goes nowhere at all. / And mind the road." Pip: "Still nothing. Milo's thinking. I can hear him thinking from here." Then the key (editor: from here Jay carries it): Pip unclips the starter key on its little gold bird keyring from her own belt and clips it to Jay's belt loop. **(keep)** Pip: "Driver holds the key." Jay: "Since when?" Pip: "Since Gran. It's a rule. I've just made it up."
5. `join: ['hob']`. `getIn: 'doughnut_van'`, objective "Take the doughnuts to Old Paloma". Pip stays in the hangar ("Banned. Also I'm guarding the parts. Also I'm having one of these.").
6. `goto: 'strand_checkpoint'`, vehicle `doughnut_van`, `stop: true`, radius 10, objective "Down the Neon Strand. There's a Welcome Check". `music: 'tension'`. Banter (Hob, first drive together; the topic is Hob himself): the club ("Me and four members. The members mostly drink tea. One of them's been dead since March, we keep him on the books for the vote."), Hob's opinion of Jay's driving ("You drive like you fly. I mean that as a warning."), and every pothole: **(keep)** on any hard knock, Hob: "That's a jam casualty, son." (use 2 or 3 variants: "Custard down." "We've lost a glazed.").
7. `spawn` four gs_staff at `strand_checkpoint`, `behavior: 'guard'`, `hostile: false`. `scene` at `strand_checkpoint`, cast gs_staff, hob, jay. Teal cones, a gazebo, smiling men. gs_staff: "Good morning! Welcome to Isla Paloma! Anything to declare? Pastries? Baked goods? Croissant-adjacent products?" Hob (who threw a tarpaulin stencilled AVIATION PARTS over the boxes at the last junction): "Aviation parts." gs_staff: "They smell of jam." Hob: "Hydraulic jam. Very technical." Then the croissant debate: Hob asks whether a croissant is a bread or a cake, and the three guards argue among themselves (one says bread, one says cake, the third says "It's a lifestyle") until the first waves the van through to stop the argument. gs_staff, as they go: "Enjoy your stay!" Hob, waving: "Lovely lads. Wrong about croissants."
8. `camera: 'wonder_wharf'`, 3 s, as the van pulls away: the big wheel turning slowly in the morning sun beside arcade row. No comment (fair play for R8).
9. `deliver: 'doughnut_van', to: 'plaza_cafe', maxDamage: 0.35, timeLimit: 240`, objective "Get the doughnuts to Ines's café before she opens (keep them in one piece)". `music: 'action'`, `checkpoint: true`. The damage count includes the first leg. Banter: Hob on Old Paloma's old folk and their orders ("Mrs Abara: two custard, one for the cat. The cat's diabetic. She knows. The cat doesn't."), on Paloma Field ("Last bit of this island Castellane doesn't own. His dad tried to buy it off my dad. My dad said no in a way I won't repeat in front of the doughnuts."), and a jam casualty line on knocks. Near the end, the old town: narrow lanes, the church tower over the roofs.
10. `music: 'hope'`. `camera: 'bell_tower'`, 3 s, the bell visible in the arch over the plaza. No comment (fair play for R9, which the player hasn't seen yet; it is a long plant).
11. `spawn` ines and odie at `plaza_cafe`, idle. `scene` at `plaza_cafe`, cast ines, odie, hob, jay. Ines takes the boxes like contraband in a war film ("Inside. Quick. The croissant man walks past at eight."). An old woman in a faded flame-orange cardigan is already at the counter, first in the queue. **(keep)** Odie: "Twelve doughnuts. The jam ones. Don't look at me like that, young man, I'm not a ghost." She pays exact and taps the counter three times (stage direction in her line: "(tapping the counter three times, as if to a car) There."). Her eye lands on the gold Firecrest keyring on Jay's belt; a long look. **(keep)** Odie: "Pretty bird." She goes, without hurrying, down into `old_lanes` (`leave: ['odie']`). Jay watches her go. Hob: "Odie Wren. Keeps the fort light. Twenty years I've done her doughnuts. Never once said thank you. Pays exact." Ines: "She said thank you once. Nineteen years ago. I wrote it down." (No calendar years for anything in the story's own timeline; Quill's historical years are fine.) Nobody mentions the taps.
12. `phone: 'milo'` (Jay and Hob by the van, Ines's queue growing). Milo opens with a fact: "Did you know Wonder Wharf's big wheel takes eleven minutes to go round and has never once gone anywhere? That's important. Definitely." In the background Quill gives his wrong answer first: "Clearly the rhyme refers to the paddle wheel of the island ferry *Constance*, which in 1931 went round in circles for an entire afternoon." Milo explains (two lines): **(keep)** "A box where nobody wins: a claw machine. A wheel that turns and spins and goes nowhere: a big wheel." "Wonder Wharf has both, side by side. The Golden Grabber. It's been there since before Grandad had eyebrows." Quill: "I had eyebrows in 1931. (beat) I wasn't born in 1931. Never mind." `if: 'dlc_blog_on'`: Milo: "I'll post it tomorrow. I wait a day now. It's agony." `else`: Milo: "It's on the wall. In pencil. In case."
13. `scene` (short) at `plaza_cafe`, cast hob, jay. Hob hands Jay a jam doughnut from the box he kept back: "Wages." Jay: "This is a doughnut." Hob: "That's how the war works, son." Pass.
- **Laugh:** "That's a jam casualty, son"; "Hydraulic jam"; the croissant debate ("It's a lifestyle"); the dead club member kept on the books; "Wages." / "This is a doughnut."
- **Callbacks:** three taps (newsreel, d03); the keyring; R8 re-read; Hob's windsock (he may grumble "new windsock, forty dollars" once).
- **Ambient text after:** none in the bible table. Optional: `text` from pip: "Hob's given me a job. I'm sorting jam from custard. I'm a Speedway champion."

Milo (phone): "Grandad got four on his driving test." Hob: "Out of ten?" Milo: "Out of a hundred." Hob gives Jay a small green logbook: STUDENT PILOT, J. MERCER, "Lesson 1. Seven. 12. `text` from hob (after the pass screen or as the last step): "Lesson 2 whenever you like. Bring your own doughnut. Hob." (This is the hook for *Marks Out of Ten*.)
